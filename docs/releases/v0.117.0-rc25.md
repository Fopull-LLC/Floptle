# Floptle v0.117.0-rc25 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc24, plus craft that come to rest on a moving planet, cheaper
physics near the ground, and a Hub that sits idle quietly.

## Resting on a moving world

A craft or vehicle parked on a planet moving on its rails never fell asleep,
however still it stood, and a big one kept costing physics time for as long
as it lay there. Carrying it along with its planet each tick rounded its
place a little differently every time, and moved it steadily away from the
middle of the physics world, where positions are coarser.

- The physics world now rides along with the planet the camera is on, so
  nothing on it is moved at all by the carry. A craft on a moving planet
  rests and sleeps exactly as it would on still ground, on the same tick and
  on the same spot.
- `node.wake` and `assembly.info(node).wake` say what last woke a body or a
  craft: `settling`, `moving`, `unsupported`, `contact`, `ground`, `joint`,
  `force`, `impulse`, `script` or `reshaped`.

## Cheaper near the ground

A distance to the ground skips the parts of the terrain too far away to
matter, with the same answer. In our tests a plane rolling on terrain costs
about a third of what it did, and a 1,000-part craft lying on the ground
about a quarter.

## The Hub

The Hub no longer runs a processor core flat out while a game it launched is
running, or while it waits for the list of versions. It checks on its work a
few times a second instead.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. What you may notice:

- Bodies on a planet moving on its rails move very slightly differently from
  before, because the carry no longer rounds their positions every tick.
- A joint anchored to a fixed point in the world, on a planet moving on its
  rails, now stays with the planet instead of being left behind in space.
