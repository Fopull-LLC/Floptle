# Floptle v0.117.0-rc32 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc31, plus a count of what terrain holds in memory, a budget to
hold it to, and generated worlds that take less memory.

## What terrain holds

`terrain.stats()` says how much memory the terrain holds, part by part: its
voxels at full detail, the coarse stand-ins for ground put away, the meshes on
the GPU and in memory, the collision surface, the shadow volume and the undo
history. Pass a terrain's id for one terrain, or nothing for all of them. It is
counted once a second while a game runs, and `age` says how old the count is.

## A memory budget

`terrain.setBudget(bytes)` sets the most memory every terrain together may
hold. While it holds more, the terrain keeps less of its far detail, the
farthest first, and takes it back once it is comfortably under the budget.
What the player can touch is always kept: the ground within 64 m at full detail
and within 120 m at no coarser than half. A budget too small for that is said
once in the Console. `terrain.stats().detail` says how much detail is kept.

## Less memory for generated worlds

The ground of a generated world now takes about an eighth less memory: each
stored chunk kept room it no longer needed once it was made.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. No budget is set unless a game sets one.
