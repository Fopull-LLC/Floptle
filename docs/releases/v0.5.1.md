# Floptle v0.5.1 — "Animator Attachments"

A focused animator stability patch: timeline editing now stays safely scoped to
the open animation, and external equipment can follow a character rig.

## Reliable timeline editing

- **Undo and redo stay in the animation.** `Ctrl+Z`, `Ctrl+Y`, and
  `Ctrl+Shift+Z` in the **Animating** tab now operate only on the open clip. They
  cannot restore a scene snapshot or undo an unrelated editor operation.
- **Copy, cut, and paste are selection-safe.** Transform-key clipboard operations
  use the current timeline selection. In particular, an empty Cut no longer acts on
  a previous clipboard selection.

## Attach equipment to an animated rig

- Any scene mesh nested below a rigged model can now be attached to one of that
  model's objects or bones from **Inspector → Bone attachment**.
- Choosing a target bone normalizes the equipment directly below the rig and keeps
  its current world pose as the bone-local offset, so a rapier, shield, emitter, or
  pickup does not jump when attached. It follows the hand through animation,
  physics, rendering, particles, and gizmo edits.
