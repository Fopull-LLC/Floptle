# Floptle v0.116.0-rc7 — "Lands On Its Feet" (beta)

Everything in rc6, with isometric blocks drawn and lit the right way round.

## Isometric blocks stand in front

On an isometric map, a tile drawn taller than its cell, like a block or a
wall, now covers the cell behind it. In rc6 the cell behind was drawn over
the block, so a row of blocks looked like a staircase of separate pieces.
Under 2D lighting, the top of each block also showed a band of the wrong
colour, because the light was worked out for the tile underneath. Both are
fixed, lit or not.
