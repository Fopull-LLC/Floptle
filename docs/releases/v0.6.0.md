# Floptle v0.6.0 — "Two of Her"

Duplicate a character and both stay themselves. This release fixes the two
animation bugs that could block a project — skinned parts bleeding between
copies of a model, and flow-rigged hair ignoring the head it's parented under —
then keeps going: real multi-select, per-object materials, beam & trail VFX,
volumetric fog, and a starter project that's actually pleasant to open.

## The blocking fixes

- **Two characters, one model, independent hair.** CPU-skinned parts used to
  bake their pose into the model's ONE shared vertex buffer — so a duplicated
  character's hair followed whichever copy animated last, and deleting the
  copy froze it. Every entity now gets its own private buffer for skinned
  parts: duplicate your fighter, animate both, each keeps their own hair.
  (Buffers are pooled and recycled, so spawning characters stays cheap.)
- **Hair follows the head now.** Skinned vertices follow *joints*, never the
  object node — so parenting a flow-rigged hair object under the Head silently
  did nothing. The engine now auto-parents a skin's loose chain root to follow
  wherever you parent its object: existing scenes pick the fix up on load with
  no asset edits, poses and keyframes replay identically — the hair just
  finally comes along. New flow-rig bakes hang the chain off the object
  directly, the Bones list gained the same "under ▸" re-parent dropdown
  objects have, and the flow-rig bake now carries the `.rig.ron` sidecar over.

## Multi-select, for real

- **Every selected object shows its outline** — not just the last one clicked
  (blobs and field shapes fold into the mask too).
- **The gizmo moves the whole selection.** Move slides everything together,
  Rotate orbits the group around the primary, Scale scales spacing too.
  Parent+child both selected? The child rides its parent once, no double-move.
- **Hierarchy drags carry the whole selection** — drop N nodes onto a new
  parent as ONE undo step (cycles and redundant children filtered).
- Viewport clicks now also accept **Ctrl** to add to the selection (matching
  the Hierarchy), alongside Shift.

## Materials, per object

- **Override one object's material inside a model.** Select a sub-object (the
  Hierarchy expands any model) and the new **◑ Material** block in its
  inspector gives just that part its own color/texture/shader — the rest of
  the model keeps its imported look. Saved per node, so two instances of the
  model can dress differently.
- **See a model's embedded materials.** Selecting a model asset now lists
  every material inside it — tint swatch, texture flag, and which objects use
  it — next to the existing animations list.
- **Filter embedded textures.** A per-model *crisp / smooth / smooth+mipmaps*
  choice (stored in the model's sidecar) finally lets you soften a model's
  baked-in textures without a Blender round trip.
- Models with no Material component now draw their **imported base colors**
  (they used to render white in-editor unless textured).

## Beams & trails

- **Beam tracks**: a new particle-track render mode that draws a ribbon from
  the effect to an endpoint — width and color from the track's curves,
  subdivided segments, an endpoint-pinned sine ripple, and UV scroll for
  energy crackle. Aim it from Lua — `node:particles():setBeamEnd(x, y, z)`
  takes a WORLD point, so a raycast laser tracks its target every tick.
- **Particle trails**: any billboard track can leave fading ribbons behind its
  particles — sword slashes, wind streaks, tracer fire. Per-track history
  time, width, taper, and its own texture; untrailed effects pay nothing.
- Both ride the existing instanced particle pass (every blend mode, fog, and
  retro come along for free), and `setIntensity`/`setBeamEnd` joined the IDE's
  Lua autocomplete + docs.

## Volumetric fog

- The Lighting node's fog gained a **mode**: keep the cheap depth ramp, or
  switch to **volumetric** — a height-bounded layer of drifting, noise-broken
  mist marched per pixel. Hills poke out of ground fog; patches roll past.
  Density, layer top, softness, noise amount/scale — all in the Lighting
  panel. Old scenes are untouched (depth stays the default).

## A better first five minutes

- **New projects open pretty**: a grassy sculpt-ready terrain, a Down gravity
  volume, three physics shapes waiting to tumble on Play, a warm sun with soft
  dithered fog — and **retro pixelation off** by default (it's one toolbar
  click away).
- The default camera's `freelook.lua` flycam is now actually shipped with new
  projects (it was referenced but never seeded).

## Editor & input fixes

- **The play-mode cursor stays put.** Camera scripts calling
  `setMouseLocked` every frame were re-issuing the OS pointer lock at frame
  rate (a flickering, uncontrollable cursor on Wayland), and egui could
  un-hide a grabbed cursor. Lock requests are deduped, the hide is
  re-asserted, and **Escape now always frees the mouse** — including from a
  script's lock.
- **Rotation gizmo spins the way you drag.** The screen-space flip was
  backwards whenever the rotation axis faced the camera.
- **Negative scale works.** The scale gizmo no longer snaps a mirrored node
  to +0.01 (magnitude floors keep the sign), the attachment offset scale
  accepts negatives, and the whole bone-attachment math moved to the scene
  graph's own componentwise TRS — a mirrored character (your player-2 fighter)
  keeps its attachments on the correct side instead of popping.
- **The script IDE highlights other occurrences** of whatever you select,
  like every other editor.

## Under the hood

- `Transform::inv_mul` — an exact componentwise inverse of the scene graph's
  transform composition (what makes mirrored parents behave).
- `Raster::clone_mesh` (per-entity skinned buffers) and
  `Raster::set_mesh_sampling` (embedded-texture filtering).
- Scene files gained `object_materials` on nodes and volumetric-fog fields on
  lighting — both fully backwards-compatible.
