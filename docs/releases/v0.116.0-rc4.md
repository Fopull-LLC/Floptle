# Floptle v0.116.0-rc4 — "Lands On Its Feet" (beta)

Everything in rc3, plus a template where people talk to you, 2D lights that
shade relief and shine from one direction, sprites that stretch without
stretching their borders, and box-shaped queries for 2D bodies.

## Pixel Village: a dialogue box you own

A new template, **Pixel Village** (`floptle new my-game --template village2d`,
or pick it on the Hub's New project screen). Walk up to someone and press E.
Their words type out in a pixel font, inside a box with their portrait, and a
question gives you choices to pick with the arrows, the d-pad or E.

None of it is engine: it is `scripts/talk.lua`, under two hundred lines, and a
conversation file per character in `data/`. Change the words, add a character,
or rewrite how it all works. The pixel font is a bitmap font made from the
engine's own pixel typeface.

## Normal maps on 2D art

Give a sprite's or a tile layer's Material a **normal map** and 2D lights shade
its relief. The side of a bump facing a lamp brightens and the side facing
away darkens. A sprite that flips or turns is still lit from the right side. A
surface without a normal map lights exactly as before.

## Suns and spot lamps in 2D

- Tick **sun** on a 2D light and it lights everything it reaches from one
  direction: the way the node's down arrow points. Rotate the node to turn
  it. Walls throw long shadows away from it. From a script:
  `node:setLighting2D{ sun = true }`.
- A lamp with **aim it (spot)** ticked now shines a cone in 2D too, aimed the
  same way.

## 9-slice sprites

Give a sprite's Material a **9-slice** border (Inspector ▸ Material ▸
**9-slice**, in texture pixels). Then scaling the node stretches the sprite
while its corners keep their pixel size and its edges stretch along their
length only. One small picture makes a platform, a sign or a window of any
size. From a script: `node:setMaterial{ slice = 8 }`.

## Box queries for 2D bodies

- `boxcast(origin, dir, halfX, halfY, max)` sweeps a box in the 2D plane and
  returns the first thing it meets. It catches a step lower than the box's
  middle, which a ray from the centre would pass over, and a box sliding
  along the floor doesn't hit the floor.
- `overlapBox(center, halfX, halfY)` lists everything a box overlaps, deepest
  first.

## Particles keep their layer

A particle system on a node with a **sorting layer** now draws its particles
at that layer. Dust kicked up behind the player stays behind the player.

## WOFF fonts

`.woff` fonts work anywhere a font goes. A `.woff2` isn't read yet. Use one
and the Console tells you how to convert it.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do, unless a 2D light already had **aim it (spot)** ticked. It used
to shine all round in a 2D scene whatever that said, and now it shines its
cone. Untick it if you want it to shine all round.
