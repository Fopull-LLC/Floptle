# Floptle v0.117.0-rc33 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc32, plus fog through plants shared like the rest of the view,
parked craft that sleep in the wind, and a count of what keeps waking them.

## Fog through plants

Since rc28 the volumetric fog is worked out once for every four pixels and
shared. Plants and other thin things missed out: nearly every pixel of a leaf
or a stem sits at an edge with the ground behind it, and each of those pixels
worked out its own fog. Now each shared sample holds two depths, the nearest
and the farthest surface of its pixels, so a leaf and the ground behind it both
find theirs. In our tests, with a dense field of thin poles in front of the
ground, the share of pixels using the shared fog went from about 94 % to over
95 %, and each pixel that shares no longer works out its own.

## Parked in the wind

Since rc28 a craft asleep under a steady force, such as wind, buoyancy or an
idle thruster, stays asleep while the force stays the same. A force that
wobbles, as wind does, woke it again and again. Now what it rests on carries
any change of up to a fifth of its weight (and the matching turn at its size),
so it sleeps through a breeze and wakes for a real push.

## What keeps waking things

`perf.counts().wakes` counts the sleeping bodies and craft woken last frame, by
cause: `{ force = 2, contact = 1 }`, with the names `node.wake` uses. A craft
woken again and again reads `settling` between wakes, and this says what keeps
waking it.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. A sleeping craft no longer wakes for a change in the force held
on it smaller than a fifth of its weight. A script that pushes a parked craft
gently and waits for it to move should push harder, or set its speed.
