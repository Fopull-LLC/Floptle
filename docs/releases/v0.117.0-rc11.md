# Floptle v0.117.0-rc11 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc10, plus big batches of terrain edits that land many a frame,
ground that streams in wherever the camera goes, and nodes that can always be
destroyed.

## Terrain edits

- A batch of big digs and flattens is made ahead in the background and lands
  many a frame, instead of one a frame. A loading screen that grades a site
  with hundreds of edits finishes several times sooner.
- `terrain.editBudget(ms)` sets how much of each frame terrain edits may take
  (4 ms by default). Raise it while a loading screen covers the view, and set
  it back after; it returns the budget it replaced.
- A small edit over ground the world has not made yet is made in the
  background too, so the first dig on a fresh world no longer stalls the frame.

## Streaming

- Ground streams in around the game camera as well as around physics bodies,
  so a rock or moon the camera flies up to gets its ground without
  `terrain.warm`. `terrain.lodAnchor` moves the camera's anchor, as before.

## Scripting

- `destroy` removes any node, including an empty root that `assembly.split`
  makes. A handle to a node that is already gone logs a warning instead of
  doing nothing silently.
- A kinematic body that a script moves from `update` while it rides a moving
  world sits one step behind its node, where rays and overlaps cannot find it.
  The Console now says so once, naming the body, and the fix: move it in
  `fixedUpdate`, or parent it to the world it stands on.
