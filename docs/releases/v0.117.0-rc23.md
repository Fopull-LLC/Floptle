# Floptle v0.117.0-rc23 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc22, plus a refresh rate that follows the window, additive
scenes that leave the running world alone, and new decals that wait for
their ground.

## Frame pacing

- `app.refreshRate()` reports the display the window is on, and follows it
  when the window moves to another one. Before the window is first shown it
  answers with the fastest display, so a frame cap set from it never holds
  a game below its display. Read it when you set the cap:
  `app.setFrameCap(app.refreshRate())`.
- When a compositor presents the window on another display's clock, the
  frame-time panel and the Console say so and name both displays.
- With Adaptive or Immediate presentation, the frame-time panel no longer
  reports dt snapping as a fault: nothing in those modes waits for the
  display, and the panel says how to hold frames to the refresh instead.
- A display path that cannot do the presentation a project asks for (such
  as Adaptive under XWayland on some drivers) falls back with a Console
  warning that says what it fell back to and what that means.

## Scenes and decals

- `scene.load(name, { additive = true })` starts only the new layer's
  sounds and effects. Before, it restarted every sound and effect already
  playing in the world. Its steps are named in `perf.parts` like a full
  scene change.
- A new decal waits for the ground under it to be ready before it is laid,
  instead of preparing that ground on the frame. A mark asked for just
  before a dig now lies on the dug ground.
- The terrain shadow's first volume after a world arrives is always made in
  the background, even when the GPU is short of memory, and the shadow work
  is named in parts.

## Profiler

- `perf.counts()` reports the terrain's field chunks: `fieldChunks` at full
  detail, `fieldEdited` dug or built, and `fieldStandIns` put away.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. A game that relied on an additive load restarting the
world's sounds or effects should start them itself.
