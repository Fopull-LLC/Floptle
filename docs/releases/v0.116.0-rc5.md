# Floptle v0.116.0-rc5 — "Lands On Its Feet" (beta)

Everything in rc4, plus 2D bodies shaped like their art, and every web font
format.

## Bodies shaped like their sprites

A Rigidbody's shape can now be **Polygon**: an outline of up to eight corners
in the 2D plane. It stands on that outline, so a character drawn from the
feet up rests with its feet on the floor, and a diamond balances on its
point. Like a box, it keeps its orientation rather than tumbling.

- **Add Component ▸ Physics ▸ Rigidbody** on a sprite traces the opaque
  pixels of the frame it shows. The body covers the character, not the
  empty space around it in its frame. A sprite whose frame is solid edge to edge and centred on its
  node still gets a box, which is already exact.
- In the Inspector, **trace from art** traces the outline again, for after
  you redraw a sprite. Each corner has an **x** and **y** you can type.
  **+ corner** splits the longest edge, **−** removes a corner, and **box**
  resets to a rectangle. **depth** is how thick it is.
- When the sprite flips, its body flips with it, and it scales with the node.
- A **Static** polygon body is a wall, floor or ramp in that shape, turned
  with its node.
- From a script: `node:setBodyOutline{ vec2(-0.4, 0), vec2(0.4, 0), vec2(0, 1) }`.
  Use it for a body that changes shape, like a crouch or a roll.

The outline is always kept convex. A corner dragged inward past its
neighbours drops out instead of making a dent.

## WOFF2 fonts

`.woff2` fonts now work anywhere a font goes: Project Settings ▸ **UI font**,
`ui.make`, `draw.text`. A font downloaded for a website works as it comes.
A damaged file, or a font collection, says so in the Console and the text
uses the built-in font.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Bodies already in your scenes keep their shapes. Only a body
you add to a sprite from now on starts as a traced polygon. If you want a
box, pick **Box** as its shape.
