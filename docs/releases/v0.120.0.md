# Floptle v0.120.0 — "Steady Light"

Reflections stay on while a game takes pictures, and spring bones settle
where the animation puts them. Ground under cover can be lit from a cache
instead of rays from every pixel, terrain costs a third of what it did to
shade, and a playtest in the editor can have the whole window.

## Reflections that stay put

- **Taking a picture no longer switches the game's reflections off.** A
  script's `camera.capture` or `camera.captureTexture` from another camera
  (an icon, a thumbnail, a portrait) used to drop the game's reflections for
  that frame. A game taking a run of thumbnails lost its mirrors, and shiny
  floors and metal flickered, for as long as that took. Each capture camera
  and size now keeps its own, and touches nothing else.

## Spring bones that settle

- **A chain shoved once comes back.** Hair, cables and tails pushed by a
  gust or a stop now return to the pose the animation holds, instead of
  standing out for good and drifting further with every shove.
- **No more shaking on a moving body.** A chain riding something at a steady
  speed read the motion as braking and surging when frames and physics steps
  fell out of step, and its hair shook. It now measures motion over the time
  that was actually drawn.
- **`anim:boneDrawn(bone)`** tells a script where a bone is drawn, swing
  included, so a script can measure a swinging hair tip.
- **`drawDt`** gives scripts the time the drawn world moved between the last
  two frames.

## Cheaper light under cover

- **A sky cache for crowded scenes.** Set a sky cache reach on the Lighting
  node (`skyCacheReach` from a script) and ambient cover at that reach is read
  from a grid kept around the camera, instead of four rays from every pixel.
  A shore or a beach full of crates and crew costs far less. Things that move
  are still covered as they move. Off by default, so existing scenes look as
  they did.

## Faster terrain

- **Terrain shades each visible pixel once,** like every other solid surface.
  Ground hidden behind nearer ground costs nothing: on a whole planet in view
  the main pass fell from about 9 ms to 3.

## Playtesting

- **A maximized tab gets the whole window.** Double-clicking the Game tab no
  longer puts a header over the top of the screen where a game draws its HUD;
  how to get back shows for two seconds and fades.
- **F11 puts the Game tab over the window and back.** In the Game tab, the
  first Escape frees the mouse and the next one restores the layout, so
  getting your cursor back never throws you out of the playtest.

## Shadows

- **Sun step,** a new Lighting setting (`env.shadowSunStep`, in degrees),
  holds the sun's shadow map still while a slow sun turns, moving it once per
  step instead of every frame. It is for a slow sun over a big world. Off by
  default.
