# Floptle v0.117.0-rc12 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc11, plus physics that costs a fraction of what it did for big
craft and vehicles on the ground, shader materials that draw together, and a
way to load a level's shaders and pictures before its first frame.

## Physics costs less, and moves the same

Every body ends where it did before, to the last bit; only the work is less.

- Each part of a craft is tested only against what it can actually reach this
  step, not everything around the whole craft. A distance question stops at
  the radius it needs, and a part in open air skips the ground entirely.
- Ground queries near terrain are several times cheaper.
- When a craft splits, only the parts near the other half are compared.
- In our tests: a 15-part plane rolling on terrain went from about 21 ms of
  physics a frame to about 4; a 1,000-part craft released onto the ground from
  53 ms to about 3, and asleep at 0.2 ms.
- Sphere, capsule and box casts skip parts far from their path, so 20 casts
  past a 1,000-part craft went from 2.1 ms to 0.2 ms. `perf.counts()` now
  reports `sweeps` and `sweepMs`.

## Drawing many of the same thing

- Parts that use the same shader material with the same settings now share
  it, so they draw together: 3,000 parts with one shader material draw once
  instead of 3,000 times. A part whose settings change gets its own copy and
  the others keep theirs. What is drawn is the same, pixel for pixel.
- Material changes are applied only to the nodes that changed, so a quiet
  frame does no material work.
- `perf.counts().unbatched` and `unbatchedBy` say how many draws could not
  be shared, and why: a unique mesh, a texture, a shader, shader settings, or
  skinning.

## No first-use freeze

- `assets.warm(list, cb)` prepares pictures, shaders, materials, models and
  sounds in the background, and calls `cb` when all are ready. Warm a level's
  assets behind its loading screen and its first frame does no first-use work.
- A shader's skinned version is built only when something skinned draws with
  it.
- Built shader pipelines are kept on disk between runs (Vulkan), so a shader
  is faster to prepare the second time.
- `perf.hitches()` names the assets a slow frame was waiting on, with their
  times: decoding, uploading, compiling a shader, or building its pipeline.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. A shader nobody warmed is still prepared the first time it
draws; call `assets.warm` on your loading screen to move that work off play.
