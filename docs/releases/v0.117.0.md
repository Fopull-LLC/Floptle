# Floptle v0.117.0 — "Whole Worlds"

Whole planets you can walk, dig, fly round and build on, with rivers,
mountain ranges and weather, that stay smooth while they stream in around
you. Craft with hinges, pistons and wheels. Water to float on. Volumetric fog
with light shafts. Meshes, textures, pictures and now compute shaders a script
makes itself. This is everything from the v0.117.0 betas, rc1 to rc38.

## Worlds

- **Planets several times the size generate in seconds**, with mountain
  ranges, rivers, lakes, rock belts and climates, and a built game can generate
  them too. Big planets hold only the ground near you and give back the memory
  they no longer need; `terrain.stats()` and `terrain.setBudget()` say what
  terrain holds and keep it to a budget.
- **Ground streams in wherever the camera goes, without stalling the frame**,
  and new ground near you is drawn exactly the way it collides. Planets save
  only what you dug.
- **Digging and marks land without a hitch.** Big batches of terrain edits are
  spread over frames under a budget a game can raise, and decals wait for the
  ground they belong on.
- **Solid ground stays where it is drawn on a world moving along its orbit**,
  through every frame, so nothing falls through and a launch pad is where it
  looks.
- **Wind and lightning** for scripts. Wind carries smoke and bends plants.
- **Water to float on and drift with.** A water volume of density 0 is there
  for its look alone, for games that float things themselves, and freezing or
  thawing water is instant.
- **Scattered props keep their model's look**, sway like the plant, and can be
  found and highlighted one at a time.

## Craft and physics

- **Craft articulate**, with hinges, pistons and gripping wheels, and keep
  their place and their hinges on a moving world.
- **Physics costs a fraction of what it did** for big craft and vehicles on the
  ground. Craft, parked vehicles and light props come to rest and fall asleep,
  in the wind or with a thruster on, and a plane lifting off over terrain costs
  a tenth of what it did.
- **A mesh becomes a tumbling physics object in one step**, every body's size
  follows its node, and a walker can no longer slip through a narrow post.
- **The floating origin distance is a project setting.**

## Looks

- **Volumetric fog worked out once for every four pixels**, shared through
  plants like the rest of the view, with **light shafts from the sun's shadow
  map** at a fraction of their old cost.
- **Shaders that read the scene behind them**, shader materials that draw
  together, and shaders that build beside play rather than in front of it.
- **Models keep their PBR maps** and the filtering their files give their
  textures. Models in any common format convert themselves with every map in
  place.
- **First-person arms and tools drawn by a stacked camera** are shaded by the
  terrain around them, and the sky keeps its sun through the fog.

## Made at runtime

- **Meshes and textures a script builds itself**, and **pictures a script
  draws** with `image.new`, many times faster than painting pixels from Lua.
- **Compute shaders**: `gpu.buffer`, `gpu.texture` and `compute.run` run
  programs on the GPU that draw nothing, for procedural textures, particle
  swarms and simulations, on every platform Floptle ships to. See
  [Compute shaders](../scripting/compute.md).
- **Players can hand a running game their own files**: a picker the game
  opens, or files dropped on its window.

## Smoother, and easier to tune

- **Saves never stall a frame or lose a player's data.** `assets.warm`
  prepares a level before its first frame, and spawns a game can spread over
  frames keep busy moments smooth.
- **A profiler that names what a slow frame did**, and new projects start with
  a catch-up budget, so an expensive moment slows the game for a moment rather
  than stuttering.
- **The refresh rate follows the window**, and scripts keep running on a busy
  machine.

## Scripting

- **Script suggestions fit what you are typing.** Node handles compare equal,
  and a destroyed node's handle stays dead, never driving a node made later.
  `findScript(kind, { quiet = true })` looks without a warning.
- **`floptle check` compiles every script**, and fails on a syntax error at its
  file and line.

## Building and the Hub

- **Exported builds draw textures the way Play does.**
- **Beta updates are one switch in the Hub** (Settings → Beta updates), and
  versions are listed in the right order. Updating to this release also moves
  a Hub that showed an old beta as the newest.

## Upgrading

Projects upgrade themselves to keep their look. Nothing to change by hand.
