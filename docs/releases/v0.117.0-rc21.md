# Floptle v0.117.0-rc21 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc20, plus an end to the stutter that decals caused on a
streamed world, crafts and vehicles that come to rest, and a smoother
take-off roll.

## No more decal stutter

A world with decals on it froze for a moment every time ground streamed in
or was dug: every mark in range was laid again in one go, and one large mark
could take tens of milliseconds.

- Ground that streams in lays again only the marks that were lying on its
  coarse stand-in, and ground that is put away lays nothing again. A dig
  lays again only the marks it reaches.
- A mark laid again is laid a piece at a time within a small budget each
  frame, like a new mark, and the old one stays on screen until the new one
  is whole.
- The ground a mark needs is prepared on a worker before the mark is laid.
- `perf.counts()` reports `decalsChecked` and `decalsRelaid`.

## Things come to rest

- A craft that broke into pieces falls asleep once its pieces have settled,
  including pieces lying against each other. Before, pieces leaning on one
  another kept each other awake forever.
- A vehicle on wheel joints falls asleep when parked: its body and wheels
  rest together and wake together.
- Pieces asleep against each other are no longer compared every step.
- `node.asleep` on a part of an assembly reads its assembly's state.
- Motion is unchanged up to the moment something falls asleep.

## Smoother take-off

A fast craft's collision ground is prepared ahead of it on a worker, so a
plane's take-off roll or a fast rover no longer builds whole terrain chunks
on the frame it reaches them.

## Scripts and tools

- `terrain.deleteSaveDir` can delete the active save from a scene with no
  terrain, such as a menu, and releases it. The refusal while a world still
  uses it says how to release it.
- The profiler names a split and a rebuild (`assemblySplit`,
  `assemblyRebuild`), times a split or rebuild callback from its own start,
  and names time lost to running out of GPU or script memory
  (`gpuOutOfMemory`, `scriptOutOfMemory`).
- `perf.pacing()` answers from the first frame.
- `floptle lint --vec3` follows scopes and statement order, so it no longer
  reports values that are not vectors.

## Upgrading

Projects upgrade themselves; nothing to do. What upgrading cannot change for
you:

- A split or rebuild callback now runs under the same per-pass time budget
  as every other script hook. A callback that ran very long before is now
  stopped like any other overrunning script.
