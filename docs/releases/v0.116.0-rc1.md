# Floptle v0.116.0-rc1 — "Lands On Its Feet" (beta)

A box dropped onto a tile floor now lands on it. Tile layers grow as you paint
and resize without moving your level, a spritesheet comes in with its
collision already worked out, and sprites dropped into a level match its
tiles.

## A 2D box stands on the floor

- **A 2D body collides in its own plane.** A box with **2D** ticked used to
  rest half sunk into a tile floor, and on a floor of small tiles, or after a
  fast fall, it went straight through. It now stands on the floor.
- **Add RigidBody on a Sprite gives a 2D box the sprite's size.** No more
  picking Box, ticking 2D and typing in a size before the first Play.
- **A RigidBody on a tilemap makes its tiles solid.** It used to turn the
  whole map into one small box at its middle, so the level looked solid and
  wasn't.
- **Making a tile solid makes its layer collide.** Marking a tile solid in
  the ◫ Tiles tab ticks **Collidable** on every layer that uses that tileset.

## Collision from your art

A spritesheet dropped on **◫ Tiles ▸ TILESET** comes in with each tile's
collision worked out from its pixels:

- a tile that is almost all art is a solid square;
- a tile whose art sits on its bottom edge with an uneven top becomes a slope
  that follows that top;
- a tile with a little art somewhere else gets a box around it;
- a nearly empty tile is walk-through.

Change any tile under **TILE**, or select some and press **fit to the art** to
work them out again.

## Room to paint

- **A layer grows as you paint past its edge.** The brush, rectangle, frame
  and line make room for themselves, and nothing already placed moves.
  Untick **GRID ▸ Grow as I paint** for a level that is a set size.
- **Resizing keeps every tile where it is.** Growing a layer used to slide
  the whole level by half the growth. New columns go on the right and new
  rows on the bottom, or on the left and top by the numbers in **anchor**.
- **Fit to tiles** shrinks a layer to the squares that hold a tile, without
  moving any.

## Sprites that fit the level

- **A dropped image lands under the pointer in a 2D view.** With the Scene
  view facing the front, a sprite dragged in from Assets used to appear in
  front of the camera rather than where you let go. It now lands on z = 0
  under the pointer.
- **A dropped sprite matches your tiles.** It takes the tiles' pixels per
  unit, so a 16-pixel character stands at the scale of 16-pixel tiles. With
  no tiles in the scene it matches the other sprites, or comes in one unit
  across.
- **Dropped sprites are unlit**, so the scene's sun does not darken them.

## Upgrading

- A 2D box that used to rest half sunk into a floor now stands on top of it,
  half its height higher.
- A tilemap with a RigidBody now collides as its tiles rather than as one box.
- Existing tilesets keep the collision you gave them. Only new ones, or tiles
  you press **fit to the art** on, are worked out from the art.
