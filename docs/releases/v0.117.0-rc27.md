# Floptle v0.117.0-rc27 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc26, plus parked vehicles and light props that fall asleep,
and a plane that no longer stalls the physics as it lifts off.

## Coming to rest

Something that rocked in place never fell asleep. A rover on light tyres and
stiff springs moved a fraction of a millimetre one step and back the next, and
a light crate on rough ground spun a little one way and then the other. Each
went nowhere, but was judged one step at a time, so each kept costing physics
time for as long as it stood there.

- A vehicle and everything jointed to it, and a body on its own, now fall
  asleep when they stay where they are for half a second, however they rock
  in between.
- A body that falls asleep stops dead. One that fell asleep still turning
  slightly was woken again on the next step.

## Lifting off

On terrain, a part of a craft a few metres above the ground looked all the way
down to the ground under it on every step to learn whether it was touching
anything. A plane climbing out cost more the higher it rose, until it was clear
of the ground: on terrain with 2 m voxels, ten times its take-off roll. It now
looks no farther than it needs to, with the same answers. In our tests, a plane
flying 6 m over that terrain costs a tenth of what it did.

## Scripts

- `node.wake` and `assembly.info(node).wake` now keep the reason that woke a
  body, such as `joint`, `contact` or `script`, until it falls asleep, instead
  of reading `settling` as soon as it held still. `settling` now means it
  stopped on its own, and `moving` that its own motion started its count again.
- A joint's anchors and axis can be read: `anchorX`, `anchorY`, `anchorZ`,
  `otherAnchorX`, `otherAnchorY`, `otherAnchorZ`, `axisX`, `axisY` and
  `axisZ` on `node:getcomponent("Joint")`.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. What you may notice:

- Parked vehicles and props that rocked in place now fall asleep. Until then
  they move exactly as before.
- A script that waited for `node.wake == "settling"` to see a body hold still
  sees the reason that woke it until the body sleeps. Read `node.asleep`
  instead.
