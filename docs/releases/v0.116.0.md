# Floptle v0.116.0 — "Lands On Its Feet"

A complete 2D engine. Start from a pixel template, bring in art from Aseprite
and levels from Tiled or LDtk, and paint square, isometric or hexagonal maps
with collision worked out from the art. Bodies collide, turn and join, and 2D
lights cast clean shadows. This is everything from the seven betas,
v0.116.0-rc1 to rc7.

## Start a 2D game

- **Pixel Platformer** (`--template platformer2d`): a tile level with slopes
  and ledges, a player who runs and jumps, and a camera that follows. **🎓
  Learn ▸ Build a 2D pixel platformer** builds it from an empty project.
- **Pixel Village** (`--template village2d`): walk up to someone and press E
  to talk, with portraits and choices. The dialogue is `scripts/talk.lua` and a
  file per character in `data/`, all yours to change.
- A scene whose camera is orthographic and faces the front opens in a 2D
  Scene view.

## Art and levels in

- **Aseprite files are sprite sheets.** Put an `.aseprite` in the project and
  you get a sliced sheet and a sprite animation per tag, rewritten each time
  you save. No export step.
- **Levels from Tiled and LDtk.** Drag a `.tmx` or `.ldtk` into the Scene view.
  Each level becomes a node with a tile layer per layer, an empty node per
  object (tagged with its class), and one tileset. Tiled's isometric and
  hexagonal maps come in with their shape.
- A spritesheet dropped on **◫ Tiles ▸ TILESET** gets each tile's collision
  worked out from its pixels: solid squares, slopes that follow the art's top,
  boxes round small art, and nothing for empty tiles.
- A dropped image lands under the pointer at the level's pixel size, unlit.
- Bitmap fonts (`.fnt`), `.ttc`, `.woff` and `.woff2` fonts work anywhere a
  font goes.

## Tile maps

- **Isometric and hexagonal.** Set a tileset's **grid** in the ◫ Tiles tab to
  square, isometric, hex (pointy top) or hex (flat top). Tiles draw in their
  cells, the cursor picks cells by their shape, and solid cells collide and
  cast shadows with their own outline. Art taller than its cell stands up out
  of it, in front of the cells behind. `tm:cellAt` and `tm:worldAt` follow the
  shape.
- **A layer grows as you paint past its edge**, and resizing never moves your
  tiles.
- **One-way platforms**: tick **one-way** on a tile. `node.dropThrough = true`
  falls through one, and `tm:oneWay` asks whether a square is one.
- **Turn and mirror while you paint**: `,` and `.` turn the brush a quarter
  turn, `[` and `]` mirror it.
- `tm:path(from, to)` finds a route round the walls of a top-down level.
- Tiles a script sets during play collide from the next frame.

## Physics that behaves like things

- **2D bodies stand on the floor.** They collide in their own plane and no
  longer sink into or fall through tile floors.
- **Bodies shaped like their sprites.** A Rigidbody added to a sprite is
  traced from its art, flips with it, and can be edited corner by corner or
  set from a script with `node:setBodyOutline`.
- **Moving bodies collide with each other**, with their mass, bounce and
  friction, so crates stack and characters push them.
- **Bodies turn.** Tick **rotates** and a crate tips off a ledge and a ball
  rolls. A stack of crates stands still.
- **Fast bodies stop at thin walls.**
- **Joints.** **Add Component ▸ Physics ▸ Joint**: hinge, fixed, distance,
  rope, spring, slider, wheel and target, with limits, motors and a breaking
  force. From a script: `node:setJoint{ kind = "rope", to = hook, length = 4 }`.
- `boxcast` and `overlapBox` answer for 2D bodies.

## 2D light and shadow

- 2D lights shade **normal-mapped** sprites and tiles.
- A 2D light can be a **sun**, and a spot lamp shines its cone in 2D.
- **Shadows come from solid tiles only**, merged into whole walls, so decor
  tiles don't cast and the streaks that fanned out from a light across tile
  floors are gone.
- **shadow strength** lets a shadow keep some light, and **height** sets how
  high a lamp hangs over normal-mapped art.
- Lit particles take the lights near them.

## The camera

- A 2D camera's limits keep everything it shows inside the level.
- **look ahead** leads the player, and **rooms** keeps the view inside the
  room the player is in.
- **art pixels** holds the zoom where every art pixel is a whole number of
  screen pixels, and Play names any sprite drawn at a different density.
- A followed player no longer wobbles a pixel.
- `scene.load("level2", { fade = 0.6 })` fades between scenes.

## Sprites and particles

- **9-slice** sprites stretch without stretching their borders.
- Particles keep their emitter's sorting layer, and a track can **collide with
  the level**: bounce, slide, or die on contact.

## Editing

- The Image tab has frame holds, tags and a wider onion skin. A marquee
  dragged in any direction selects its box, and **Ctrl+wheel** sizes the
  brush.
- In the Scene view, drag a selected node to move it. A 2D node rotates only
  in its plane.
- Any type added to a node can be removed with **Remove component**.
- Tileset and particle edits undo with Ctrl+Z.

## Tools

- `floptle run` and `floptle shot` take `--fps` and `--frame-jitter` to
  reproduce frame-rate bugs headless.
- A scene param pinned as a number that a script has turned into an on/off
  switch is named in the Console and by `floptle check`.

## Upgrading

- A 2D box that used to rest half sunk into a floor stands on top of it.
- A tilemap with a Rigidbody collides as its tiles, not as one box.
- A 2D camera with limits stops half a view further in from each edge. If you
  set your limits inside the level to make up for it, set them back to the
  level's edges.
- A 2D light with **aim it (spot)** ticked now shines its cone.
- A tile layer casts 2D shadows only from its solid tiles. Mark decor tiles
  solid if you want them to cast.
- Bodies already in your scenes stay upright unless you tick **rotates**.
- Image tab documents with holds or tags are saved in a newer format that
  earlier versions won't open.
