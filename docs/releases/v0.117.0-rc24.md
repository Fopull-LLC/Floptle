# Floptle v0.117.0-rc24 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc23, plus scattered props a game can find and highlight one
at a time, and beams that bow.

## Scattered props, one at a time

- `scatter.instance(source, id)` finds one prop by the id `scatter.near`
  gave it, where it has settled: its position, up, yaw, scale and param.
  It answers nil once the prop is removed.
- `scatter.setTint(source, id, { tint, emissive, emissiveStrength, rim,
  rimStrength })` highlights one prop, and `scatter.setTint(source, id, nil)`
  clears it. `scatter.clearTints(source)` clears every highlight on a
  source. A highlight changed every frame costs nothing extra, and a source
  with none costs nothing.

## Effects

- A beam can bow: give its track a control point in the Particles tab, or
  call `ps:setBeamControl(point)`, and the beam curves from its start to
  its end, pulled toward the point. With no point it stays straight.
- `ps:setVolume(v)`, and `volume` in `spawnEffect`, scale the space an
  effect fills without changing its particles' size, number, speed or
  brightness: the same smoke can fill a room or a cup.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Effects saved before this load and save unchanged.
