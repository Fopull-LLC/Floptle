# Floptle v0.117.0-rc31 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc30, plus shade for first-person arms and tools.

## First-person views in the shade

Arms and a held tool drawn by a stacked camera (see `docs/camera-stacks.md`)
were lit as in open sun wherever the player stood: in a cave, or in the
shadow of a hill. Now the terrain shades them, as it shades everything else
around the player.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. A first-person view model in the shade of terrain now draws
darker, as it always should have.
