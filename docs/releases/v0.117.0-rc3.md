# Floptle v0.117.0-rc3 — "Whole Worlds" (beta)

Everything in rc2, plus water to float on, wind that moves things, planets that
save only what you changed, and craft that hold together on a moving world.

## Water to float on

`water.addVolume{ shape = "lake", center = c, radius = 40 }` declares a body of
water, and `shape = "heightfield"` takes a grid of levels for a river or a
terraced pond. A volume given a `parent` moves with its planet. Bodies float
on it, feel its `drag`, and drift with its `current`. `water.flowAt(point)`
reads the current, and `water.at` now says which volume a point is in.
`water.surface(id)` gives the mesh of exactly the surface the water answers
for, ready for `mesh.build`, so what you see is what things float on.

A surface shader can read what is behind it with `sceneColor(uv)` and
`sceneDepth(uv)`, and `screenUv(p)` gives the coordinates to read at, for
refraction and water that darkens with depth.

## Wind that moves things

A particle track can opt in to the game's wind, so smoke and dust drift with
`weather.addWind`. A material's **sway** bends a mesh or a scattered prop
downwind from its root, and **backlight** lets the sun through thin leaves.
Beams take a seeded `jitter` for lightning and electric arcs, thrown again on
each pulse.

## Planets save only what you changed

A generated planet now saves its edits and how it was generated, not the whole
field. A dug planet that saved 536 MB saves under 1 MB. Loading regenerates the
planet and lays the edits over it, so a load still takes about as long as
generating.

Rivers follow the land down to the sea or a basin, and never run in circles.
`terrain.planetRivers` and `terrain.planetLakes` take a callback as their last
argument to answer without holding up the script. Coarse ground far away keeps
to the real surface: a bowl no longer draws as a peak, and dug ground is always
drawn at full detail. A bite out of the ground costs a few milliseconds of the
frame, and a planet seen from orbit takes a fraction of the draws.

## Craft that hold together

- A craft rebuilt mid-flight keeps its place and its velocity, fast or
  spinning.
- A push from a rebuild callback lands at the centre of mass, so a light craft
  hitting a moving planet no longer bounces off at hundreds of metres a second.
- Bodies and craft spawned on a moving planet land where they were placed, and
  a body moved while paused goes where it was sent.
- A hinge between two parts of a craft on an orbiting planet pivots where it
  was made.
- Joints gain a `ball` kind with a cone limit, and wheels can steer with
  `steer`. A braked rover holds still on any slope its tyres can grip.
- A switched-off body rides its planet, and `node.vx` reads true on a craft far
  from the camera.
- A small rock carries what sits on it. `space.beltOrbits{...}` gives a seeded
  belt of orbits to place rocks on.

## Smaller things

- Scripts no longer pause every few seconds to rebuild their copy of a scene
  that is streaming nodes in and out.
- `anim:boneWorld`, read before any write in a frame, gives the animator's
  pose, not last frame's write.
- `input.commitRebind` replaces the old binding, and `input.saveBindings`,
  `loadBindings` and `resetBindings` keep what a player chose. The settings
  page lists each monitor once, and `ui.engineSettings` can sit inside a game's
  own screen.
- Scattered props settle a few at a time without hitches, `scatter.near` is
  right from the frame a source is made, and shots show a settled field.
- Particles at rest stay at rest while terrain streams in nearby.
- A shader with texture slots no longer fails to build, and `floptle check`
  says when one has more textures than the GPU allows.
- Models that use the same image share one copy of it.
- `floptle shot` draws glass and scene-reading shaders the way the editor does.

## Upgrading

Nothing to do. Four things behave differently:

- `input.commitRebind()` now replaces the binding it is rebinding. Pass `"add"`
  to keep the old one as well.
- `anim:boneWorld` read before a frame's writes no longer includes last frame's
  writes. Use `anim:holdPose(true)` to keep a pose.
- A planet saved by this version stores only its edits, and is laid over the
  generator on load. Planets saved by older versions load as before.
- Rivers are traced differently, so a world generated again with the same
  options can have shorter rivers in different places.
