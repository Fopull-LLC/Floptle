# Floptle v0.112.0 — "Moves With You"

A game on a planet that orbits now plays as if the ground held still: smoke
stays over its spot, marks stay on the rock, and nothing jitters against the
world it stands on. Caves read as rock, a craft no longer gets flung by its own
pilot, phones scroll and steer properly, and smoke can be lit.

## Effects ride the world they are on

On a planet moving on rails, particles used to stay where they were in space
while the ground moved on, a metre and a half every tick, and they jittered
against it because they moved on a different clock.

- **`spawnEffect(key, pos, opts)`** takes a table: `normal` (the effect's up,
  so a ring lies on the ground and a cone fires off it), `up`, `scale`,
  `intensity`, `tint`, `vel` and `frame`. The old
  `spawnEffect(key, x, y, z [, vx, vy, vz])` still works.
- An effect rides its `frame`, a planet or a ship: where it emits from, and
  every particle it has already made, move and turn with it. With no frame it
  rides the planet whose sphere of influence it is in.
- A point your script works out in `update` lands where you meant on the
  planet, even though the planet moves before the effect is spawned.
- A node's own effect rides the planet it hangs under.
- Gravity on a Local-space track falls toward the ground, even on a node tipped
  sideways on a round world.
- Upright and flat billboards stand on the local ground, not world up.
- **`decals.add{ …, frame = node }`**: a mark rides what it was laid on. Without
  a frame it rides the planet it is on, so a scorch stays under its rock.

## Smoke that takes the light

- **Lit billboards.** Tick **lit** on a billboard track and each puff takes the
  sun (or the brightest star) and the ambient light, rounded like a ball. Over
  a planet's night side it gets only the ambient, so smoke darkens at dusk
  instead of glowing.
- **Random between two curves** for size and colour: every puff in a track
  grows, but not alike. It is the 🎲 on a curve in the Inspector.
- **`node:particles():setTint(r, g, b [, a])`** and `spawnEffect`'s `tint`
  colour an effect, so one dust effect matches each world's ground.
- **Blend frames**: a flipbook can crossfade each frame into the next instead
  of stepping.
- **Distortion (heat haze)**: a blend mode that bends the scene behind a
  particle by its texture, with a **strength**.
- Particles are their own line in `perf.gpu()`.

## Caves that read as rock

Generated caves are rounded tunnels and rooms with smooth junctions and a
metre of texture on their walls, instead of flat slanted sheets meeting in
dead-straight creases. Levelling ground for a building over a cave no longer
fills the cave and leaves a flat wall in it. A narrow passage that runs into
distant terrain is closed off there, rather than showing a window into the
rock.

## Physics you can trust with a crew aboard

- **A craft no longer leaps or flips from its own contacts.** A body pressed
  into an assembly (a seated pilot, a crate in a hold) could move it metres or
  turn it over in a single step.
- **`physics.ignorePair(a, b [, seconds])`** lets two bodies or assemblies
  pass through each other, for good or for a while: a passenger inside the
  ship, or debris kept clear of the hull it broke from.
- **Switching a node off stops its body.** `node.enabled = false` takes a
  moving body or an assembly out of the simulation, as the docs always said,
  and switching it on carries on from where it stopped.
- `floptle shot --after` plays the same game `floptle run` does.

## Phones

- A finger is never also the mouse. Pushing an on-screen stick no longer turns
  a mouse-look camera, and a look drag no longer turns it twice as fast.
- **A finger scrolls a list**, from anywhere in it: a label, a button or a
  slider. Drag along the list and it scrolls, and the button you started on
  does not click. A tap still clicks, and a slider in the list still slides
  sideways.

## Smoother play

- **Digging is cheaper.** In play, the ground a dig changes is rebuilt in the
  background, with the old ground shown until the new is ready, and collision
  rebuilds only what the dig reached. A mining laser biting eight times a
  second no longer costs most of a frame on every bite.
- **A slow frame always says where its time went.** `perf.ms("other")` is the
  time no other bucket measured, and `perf.hitches()` keeps every frame of
  50 ms or more, even one the buckets cannot explain.
- **A star too faint to see costs nothing.** In stars mode, a star giving less
  than a hundredth of the brightest one's light is left out of the lighting, so
  a room lit by one star no longer pays for four.
- A planet a script creates is listed in `space.bodies()` before your next
  `update`.

## Upgrading

- **Worlds generated from a seed get the new caves.** A save that remembers a
  spot inside a generated cave may find rock there.
- **`spawnEffect`'s velocity is now relative to the planet it rides.** If you
  added the planet's own velocity to keep puffs with it, stop: they ride it now.
- If a script switched off a node to freeze its body and relied on it still
  moving, that body now stops.
- Upright and flat billboards on a round world now stand on its ground.
- Nothing else to do.
